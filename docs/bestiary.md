species belongs to. This document is the authored source of truth; `data/creatures.json` remains
the small, game-facing encounter table until the two are reconciled (see
[Open Questions](#open-questions)).

## Open Questions

Decisions to settle before this content reaches the game:

1. **Region-to-biome mapping.** The seven sections are *regions*, but the generator classifies
   tiles into the ten *biomes* in `data/biomes.json`. Each species needs one or more biome tags
   before it can be placed. Some sections map cleanly (Section 1 → wetland, coast, river);
   Sections 6 and 7 have no biome equivalent at all yet.
2. **Section 1 contains strays.** Entries 43–50 of its fauna list describe Vindhya, Gedrosian,
   Mappa Mundi, lava-pool, and sky species, which belong to Sections 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6
   respectively. Captured verbatim here; they should be re-filed when the data is structured.
3. **Duplicate binomial.** *Riftia* Lava-Vent Tubeworm appears twice in Section 5 (entries 11 and
   27) as `vulcanica` and `asurica`.
4. **Tone.** The cozy pillar in [game-plan.md](game-plan.md) frames creatures as neighbours to be
   observed rather than fought. Sections 5 and 7 are predatory and horror-leaning. Decide whether
   they are encounterable, journal-only lore, or gated behind a later mode.
5. **Scale.** 300 species (230 fauna, 70 flora) against an MVP asking for six. The encounter table
   needs rarity tiers so a short session surfaces a handful, not a catalogue.
6. **Flora has no game role yet.** Nothing in the code models plants — `data/biomes.json` has no
   vegetation field and the journal only describes terrain. The 70 flora entries need a purpose
   (foraging, journal flavour, biome dressing) before they can be data.
