# Sprite heights

Generated by `node tools/measure-sprites.js`. Do not edit by hand.

Every sprite is measured from the **bottom** of its cell, because that is where things
stand. `top` is the first row that has any opaque pixel in it, so a 32-pixel cell with a
top of 21 holds an eleven-pixel plant.

## Why the heights matter

The traveller is 40 pixels in a 32-pixel cell and sorts into the `walker` slot of his row.
What draws over him and what draws under him is the difference between wading through
grass and sinking into the ground, and it is decided two ways:

* **Height** bounds what the common overdraw may do. Nothing in `overdraw.png` may start
  above row 16 of 32 -- grass to the knee reads as depth, grass to the chest loses the
  character behind it. `test/frames.test.ts` asserts this against the built sheet.
* **What the thing is** decides the rest, and height alone is the wrong rule. Moss is three
  pixels and lies flat; sagebrush is ten and is waded through. Lotus pads are the tallest
  thing on the feature sheet at sixteen pixels and lie flat on water. So the underfoot set
  is a list, and this table is the evidence for what belongs in it.
