# Play Test Guide

Open `src/index.html` in a browser to play the current prototype.

## Controls
- Generate a new route by typing a journey seed and selecting **Generate map**.
- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**.
- Sea tiles are blocked, so use coasts, rivers, plains, forests, wetlands, hills, mountains, desert, and settlements to navigate.

## Goal
Reach the `✦` landmark and record it in the travel journal. The journal updates after every move with the current biome, a gentle location description, creature signs, a plant growing there, and the discovery count.

## What To Observe
- Does the generated geography feel readable at a glance?
- Does the settlement start feel safe and welcoming?
- Can you understand which terrain is blocked or walkable?
- Does the landmark give the session a calm, finishable objective?
- Do the creature and plant suit the terrain they appear on, or do they read as misplaced?
- Does revisiting a tile show the same creature and plant as before?
